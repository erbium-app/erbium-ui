---
title: "Thumb Collision"
story: ThumbCollision
---

Set what happens when one thumb is dragged into another. `thumbCollisionBehavior="push"` (the default) moves the neighbour along, `"swap"` lets the dragged thumb pass and take over the other value, and `"none"` stops it at the neighbour. `minStepsBetweenValues` keeps thumbs a number of steps apart. It applies to linear sliders without `steps`.
