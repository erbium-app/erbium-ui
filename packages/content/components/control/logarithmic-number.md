---
title: "Logarithmic Number"
story: LogarithmicNumber
---

`scale="log"` spreads values by ratio, so a frequency or zoom row gives the low end as much room as the high end. It works in both modes: discrete rows round onto `step`, continuous rows keep four significant digits. Arrow keys move 1% of the row (10% with Shift). The value editor still accepts any in-range number, and a log scale needs `min` above zero.
