This page covers Erbium’s styled Editable. Build your own design with [unstyled Editable from `@erbium-ui/react`](/docs/react/editable) to reuse its draft state, keyboard behavior, and focus management.

## Usage

A single-line inline editor. The preview stays in the layout while the input overlays it, keeping the text position and neighboring content stable during editing. Enter or blur saves; Escape cancels.
