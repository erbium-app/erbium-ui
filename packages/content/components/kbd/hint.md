title: "Hint"
story: Hint
---

Pass `hotkey` to `Button` to register a shortcut that clicks it. While the user holds one of the shortcut's modifiers, `KbdHint` shows the shortcut in a `Badge` centred above the button, in both left-to-right and right-to-left layouts. A `ButtonShortcut` without its own `hotkey` labels the button's shortcut instead, and the badge stays hidden. The row keeps room above it for the badges.
