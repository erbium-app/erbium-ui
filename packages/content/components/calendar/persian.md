---
title: "Persian (Jalali) Calendar"
story: Persian
---

Install `@daypicker/persian` and pass its date library and locale to `Calendar`. The add-on sets the Jalali months and Persian numerals; `Calendar` keeps the library styling and lays out right to left with `dir="rtl"`. The Hijri, Hebrew, Buddhist, and Ethiopic add-ons work the same way.
