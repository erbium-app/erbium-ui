`logoSize` clears the middle of the code, and error correction recovers what it covers. An `Icon` fills the space as it is. Give an `<image>` or your own `<svg>` `width="100%"` and `height="100%"`. Downloads include it. This one draws the Erbium mark by passing its `viewBox` and paths.
