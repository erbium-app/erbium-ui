---
title: "Dots"
description: "A compact stepper drawn as the step indicator's dots."
---

Render `StepperDots` instead of `StepperList` when step names would crowd the layout, as in a tour or a questionnaire. Each enabled step is a dot that follows the flow: the current one stretches into a pill, done steps fill, and a step the flow cannot reach yet is blocked. Name the steps with `label`; each name is followed by the step's status.
