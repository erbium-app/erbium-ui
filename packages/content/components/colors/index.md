---
title: Colors
description: "Semantic colors for content and feedback."
---

Semantic tokens cover backgrounds, text, borders, and feedback. Use semantic colors such as `background`, `foreground`, `primary`, and `muted` so your interface follows the shared theme. Feedback colors provide matching foregrounds for success, warning, error, and information states.

The swatches below render the same theme tokens used by Erbium components. Compare their foregrounds and backgrounds when choosing a role for your interface.

Default filled actions use neutral `primary` colors. Use `link` for links, `selection` and `selection-foreground` for checked or selected controls, and `ring` for keyboard focus. Links, selection, and focus retain the Vitesse green independently of the default action fill. `accent` remains the neutral hover background.

Feedback colors keep separate solid and soft foreground pairs. Tag colors represent categories, and chart colors represent data series; keep those roles separate from action and status colors even when their values match. Surface tokens continue to describe elevation and interaction states.
