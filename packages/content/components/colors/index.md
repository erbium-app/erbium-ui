title: Colors
description: Semantic color tokens for backgrounds, text, borders, and feedback.
---

Use semantic colors such as `background`, `foreground`, `primary`, and `muted` so your interface follows the shared theme. Feedback colors provide matching foregrounds for success, warning, error, and information states.

The swatches below render the same theme tokens used by Erbium components. Compare their foregrounds and backgrounds when choosing a role for your interface.
