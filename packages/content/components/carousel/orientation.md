---
title: "Orientation"
story: Orientation
---

Set `orientation="vertical"` to scroll on the vertical axis. Give `CarouselSlides` a height. The up and down arrow keys move between slides.

```tsx
<Carousel aria-label="Numbers" orientation="vertical">
  <CarouselSlides className="h-72">
    <CarouselSlide>...</CarouselSlide>
    <CarouselSlide>...</CarouselSlide>
  </CarouselSlides>
</Carousel>
```
