Typography starts with readable body text. Font choice, size, line spacing, and line length work together; use them to establish a clear hierarchy before adding emphasis. [Practical Typography](https://practicaltypography.com/typography-in-ten-minutes.html) explains these fundamentals.

Erbium shares font roles across its interfaces and documentation. The examples below show them in controls, code, reading content, and multilingual layouts. [**Shadcn Typeset**](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/typeset) provides the reading styles; components keep their own text sizes and spacing.
